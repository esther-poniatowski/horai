# Hierarchy of work

> [!QUESTION] Goal: under which result or which stream is each task placed, so that no task competes for attention on its own account?

Work is ordered on four levels, which every profile names in the terms of the method. ^levels

1. the **purpose**, which spans the strategic horizon;
2. the **deliverable**, a result handed to the world or to a party;
3. the [milestone](milestone.md), in the phases that admit one, or the **question** of an [exploration](phase.md);
4. the **task**.

A task has no independent existence: it serves one parent of level 3, from which it takes its stream, or it is a loose task, which names its stream directly. ^parent-rule

- **An orphan task is not a commitment.** A task that names neither a parent nor a stream is placed in a holding file. The task is archived at the `orphan_cycles`-th cycle planning held since its entry into that file, unless that planning selects it. ^orphan-rule
- **An idea is not a task.** An idea of a possible deliverable is recorded in an ideas file, which is reviewed at the strategic horizon and never archived, since an idea can mature over years. ^ideas-file
- **Recurring work and loose actions are loose tasks.** A **loose task** serves no milestone and no question, and names its stream directly, so that the rule above holds without invented milestones. A loose task that recurs states its rule, a default one, daily, weekly or per cycle, or a period such as every two cycles, and opens again once that many periods have begun since its last completion; a one-off loose task waits in the backlog until a weekly review commits it. Every folder keeps its actions, its milestones, its questions and its loose tasks, in one **store of actions**, placed beside the files that they touch, under the same file name in every folder. Recurring work, such as a practice of mathematics, the housekeeping, the fixes and the one-off steps of a folder, and the register of debt of a stream that yields means are loose tasks of their stream. ^standing-parent
- **Finite work outside a deliverable is a milestone without a deliverable.** A group of tasks around one event, such as the follow-up of a conference, closes, so it is held by a milestone that omits its deliverable. That milestone serves its stream directly, and closes by its [definition of done](milestone.md#^definition-of-done), the closure of its tasks when it states no criterion.
- **Boundaries of the levels.** A level is told from the next by what is committed at it, after the project and the next action of Getting Things Done, the scope and the task of Shape Up, and the work package of a breakdown structure. ^boundaries
  - A **milestone**, or a question, is a result with a test of done, budgeted and finished within one cycle; it is split when it exceeds the capacity of its stream in a cycle.
  - A **task** is one action, committed for a week and offered by the selection, startable without further planning, and done in one block or a few; a task that needs a plan of its own is a milestone.
  - A **step** is a check inside one task, never selected, committed or budgeted on its own, and steps hold no steps; a step that needs its own date, dependency or selection becomes a task.
- **A deadline can attach at any level.** A purpose, a deliverable or a milestone can bear a [deadline](deadline.md). The work that the deadline covers is the remaining work of the items below it.
