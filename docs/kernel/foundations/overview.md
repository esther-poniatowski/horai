# Overview of the kernel

> [!QUESTION] Goal: which question does the method of `horai` answer, and which objects, processes and commitments does its answer comprise?

## The question that the method answers

One agent directs its own attention over work of several kinds: it delivers results, builds the means of producing them, watches its environment, maintains its competence, answers to other parties, discharges duties, and responds to requests. Situations of this [class](problem-class.md) share three failures:

- the building of means absorbs the attention owed to the results;
- work whose payoff is deferred receives none;
- open-ended work admits no reliable estimate of duration.

A task inventory corrects none of them, as the [diagnosis](diagnosis.md) states. The method therefore answers one root question: ^root-question

> How can the heterogeneous work of one agent be organized so that the next act, the attention that it receives, and the moment to stop are each fixed by a stated rule, at every time scale?

## Parts of the answer

1. **Control questions.** The root question divides into nine [control questions](control-questions.md), from classification to adaptation.
2. **Dimensions.** The problem varies along the [dimensions](dimensions.md) that the objects of the kernel represent.
3. **Objects.** A plan states [streams](../objects/stream.md) under [regimes](../objects/regime.md), the [availability](../objects/availability.md) of the agent, a [hierarchy of work](../objects/work-hierarchy.md) whose pivot is the [milestone](../objects/milestone.md), [phases](../objects/phase.md), [dependencies](../objects/dependency.md), [external parties](../objects/external-party.md), [commitment states](../objects/commitment-state.md), [deadlines](../objects/deadline.md), [appetites](../objects/appetite.md), [values](../objects/value.md) and [horizons](../objects/horizon.md).
4. **Processes.** The [processes](../processes/_index.md) allocate capacity, link the horizons, select the next task, bound enablement, control uncertainty, bound reactive work, coordinate with external parties, test imposed deadlines, and tune the estimates.
5. **Commitments.** Ten [commitments](commitments.md) justify the rules.
6. **Configuration.** Every numerical constant is a [parameter](parameters.md), and the method is installed as a [minimal core](core-and-options.md) to which options are added.

> [!WARNING] Scope of the claim
> The method is a starting configuration and not an optimum: its numerical parameters are defaults, and only the [research profile](../../profiles/research.md) has been elaborated for use. The [status of the evidence](evidence-status.md) rates each mechanism.

## Reading path

1. The [class of problems](problem-class.md), the [control questions](control-questions.md) and the [dimensions](dimensions.md).
2. The [commitments](commitments.md), the [layers](layers.md) and the [core and options](core-and-options.md).
3. The [objects](../objects/_index.md), in the order of their index, then the [processes](../processes/_index.md).
4. The [comparison of instances](../../profiles/comparison.md), then the profile of the domain concerned.
5. The [bootstrap procedure](../../procedures/bootstrap.md), which installs the method.
