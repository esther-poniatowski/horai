# Procedures

Guides the performance of each recurring act of the kernel, once the [rule](../kernel/processes/_index.md) that it applies is fixed. The procedures of a [module](../modules/_index.md) are stated with that module.

## Installation

- [Bootstrap](bootstrap.md): installs the method on an existing inventory of tasks, from the choice of a profile to the first cycle

## Planning

- [Cycle planning](cycle-planning.md): selects the milestones and the questions of a cycle, and assigns their budgets
- [Weekly review](weekly-review.md): reads the metrics, writes the evidence note and commits the tasks of the week
- [Daily planning](daily-planning.md): fixes the objectives of a day from the committed week

## Decisions during the work

- [Checkpoint decision](checkpoint-decision.md): decides the fate of a piece of work whose appetite is consumed
- [Enablement request](enablement-request.md): decides whether and how far a means is built when the work appears blocked

## Coordination

- [Request triage](request-triage.md): decides the outcome of an unplanned request at its arrival
- [External request](external-request.md): states a request to an external party, with its follow-up and its fallback
- [Deadline feasibility](deadline-feasibility.md): tests a date fixed from outside before it is accepted, and at each cycle
