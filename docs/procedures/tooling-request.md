# Tooling request

> [!QUESTION] Goal: when the work appears blocked by a missing means, what is built, how far, and under which budget?

The request applies the rules of [tooling](../kernel/processes/tooling.md).

1. **Artifact.** Name the artifact that the consumer needs, and the consumer itself: a milestone, or a kind of request that recurs.
2. **Result test.** If the means is itself delivered and valued, treat it as a milestone of production and leave this procedure.
3. **Route.** Build a durable means only when the cost of verifying and maintaining it is below the cost of producing the artifact by hand or by a means made for the occasion. Otherwise produce the artifact without a durable means, and stop here.
4. **Make or obtain.** Where the valuation is quantified, compare the cost of building with the cost of obtaining the means from outside.
5. **Slice.** State the smallest end-to-end path that produces the artifact for this consumer.
6. **Budget.** If the consumer is a milestone of the cycle, charge an appetite to it and drive the slice without interruption. Otherwise schedule the slice within the ceiling of the stream.
7. **Close.** Close the request when the consumer is unblocked. Record each generalization left aside under the standing parent of debt.
