# Comparison of instances

> [!QUESTION] Goal: what do the instances of the problem share, and on which dimensions do they differ?

The levels below are qualitative judgments, and none is measured.

## Streams by what they yield

| Yields | Research | Solo founder | Independent developer | Author | Consultant |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Results | results of papers | product | features | manuscript | client deliverables |
| Means | research tools | internal tooling | build and infrastructure | note system, toolchain | templates, process |
| Knowledge | bibliography | customer and market discovery | ecosystem watch | source reading | market watch |
| Competence | mathematics | new skills | new techniques | craft | upskilling |
| Coordination | supervision, lab meetings | investors, partners | releases, community | editor, publicity | proposals, client meetings |
| Continuity | vault, defects, planning | administration, planning | dependencies, debt, planning | administration, planning | accounting, planning |
| Triggered by arrival | correspondence | customer support | issues, contributions | correspondence | client requests |
| Obligation | teaching, if assigned | — | — | — | — |

## Modulators

| Modulator | Research | Solo founder | Independent developer | Author | Consultant |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Reactive load | low | medium | high | low | medium to high |
| External dependence | medium: supervisor, co-authors, reviewers | medium: customers, contractors | medium: upstream projects, contributors | medium: editor, interviewees | high: clients, subcontractors |
| Imposed deadlines | few, hard: submissions | few: the runway, as a hard deadline on the purpose | rare | one, contractual, on the purpose | many, contractual |
| Time sold or owed | none, or teaching | none | none | none | retainers, time sold |
| Delegation | automated agents | contractors | rare | rare | subcontractors |
| Accountability | one | several investors, or none | none, or a sponsor | one | one per engagement |
| Quantifiable cost | no | yes: revenue and burn | rarely | no | yes: fees and penalties |

## Types of deliverable

| Dimension | Research | Solo founder | Independent developer | Author | Consultant |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Types of deliverable | `abstract`, `article`, `poster`, `talk`, `chapter`, `software` | `offer`, `release` | `prototype`, `release` | `chapter`, `article` | `proposal`, `engagement` |

The phases keep the names of the kernel in every profile.

## Characteristic failure

Each instance has its form of the displacement of ends by means, and a second failure that its dominant modulator produces.

| Profile | Means displace ends | Second failure | Rule of the kernel that bears on it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Research | tools built for days without a result | learning and reading receive no time | [pull rule](../kernel/processes/tooling.md#^pull-rule), [minimums](../kernel/processes/capacity-allocation.md#^floors) |
| Solo founder | a platform built before the product is wanted | discovery stops once building starts | [phase](../kernel/objects/phase.md), [stop by value](../kernel/processes/uncertainty-control.md#^stop-by-value) |
| Independent developer | an engine built instead of the application | the queue of issues becomes the roadmap | [reserve and triage](../kernel/processes/reactive-work.md) |
| Author | a note system refined instead of the text | reading without end before drafting | [pull rule](../kernel/processes/tooling.md#^pull-rule), [phase](../kernel/objects/phase.md) |
| Consultant | templates polished between engagements | all capacity is sold, and the minimums vanish | [feasibility test](../kernel/processes/imposed-deadlines.md), [minimums](../kernel/processes/capacity-allocation.md#^floors) |

## Domains at the boundary

The second review tested further domains against the kernel:

| Domain | Fit | What it required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Doctoral researcher with teaching | fits | a stream that yields obligation, triggered by appointment |
| Clinician-scientist | fits, near the boundary | the trigger by appointment for the rota, `immediate` requests for on-call, and the check of `discretion_min` |
| Parent-carer with a side project | fits | the [availability](../kernel/objects/availability.md) with a short minimum block, and slots in place of blocks |
| Freelance designer | fits | several parties to whom the agent is accountable, review loops and dates set by clients |
| Principal investigator of a lab | partly | the relation `delegates` covers the work handed to members; the allocation of the work of the lab is outside the kernel |

## What the comparison shows

- **Every yield other than obligation occurs in every instance.** Obligation occurs where it is owed to an affiliation, such as assigned teaching in research. The differences lie in the triggers, the allocations and the modulators, which supports their separation in the [dimensions](../kernel/foundations/dimensions.md).
- **Each modulator dominates one instance.** Reactive load dominates the developer, deadlines and cost the consultant, cost and a single hard deadline the founder. Research and authorship are the instances closest to the [minimal core](../kernel/foundations/core-and-options.md#^core).
- **The minimums are hardest to keep where the worth is cardinal.** A billable hour has an observed price and an hour of training has none, which is the case that the ninth [commitment](../kernel/foundations/commitments.md#^c-valuation) addresses.
