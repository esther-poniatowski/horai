# Author

> [!QUESTION] Goal: which values configure the kernel for the author of a long work, who answers to an editor and to one contractual date?

> [!WARNING] Sketch
> This profile tests the generality of the kernel. No use has validated its values.

## Streams

| Stream | Role | Regime | Capacity |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Manuscript | production | `planned` | share, the largest of the profiles |
| Note system and toolchain | tooling | `pulled` | ceiling, low |
| Source reading | survey | `floor` | floor |
| Craft | training | `floor` | floor |
| Editor and publicity | exchange | `appointed` | cap |
| Administration | upkeep | `batched` | slots |
| Correspondence | exchange | `reactive` | reserve, small |

## Names

| Kernel | Author |
| --- | --- |
| Purpose · deliverable | book · part or chapter |
| Exploration · validation · delivery | research · argument · drafting and revision |
| Cycle | synchronized with the deliveries to the editor |

## Options

| Option | Setting |
| --- | --- |
| Reactive work | on, light |
| External parties | on: the editor is the principal (`reviews`, `imposes`); interviewees and archives (`supplies`, with a latency) |
| Imposed deadlines | on: the delivery of the manuscript is a `hard` deadline on the purpose; the deliveries of parts agreed with the editor are deadlines on deliverables |
| Valuation | ordinal |

## Notes specific to the domain

- **One buffer for the manuscript.** The deadline lies on the purpose, so its [buffer](../kernel/objects/deadline.md#^buffer) pools the margins of all chapters, and the deliveries of parts are tested with it in the [cumulative test](../kernel/processes/imposed-deadlines.md#^feasibility-test).
- **Reading has no natural end.** The passage from research to argument is a [transition of phase](../kernel/objects/phase.md) decided at a cycle planning. Afterwards, the reading that a chapter needs is charged to the chapter, in the stream Manuscript, and the floor of the stream Source reading continues for reading not tied to a chapter.
- **Interviews release waits.** A request for an interview is made before the drafting that could run in parallel, by the [release key](../kernel/processes/task-selection.md#^release-key).
