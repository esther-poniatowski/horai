# Solo founder

> [!QUESTION] Goal: which values configure the kernel for the founder of an early company, who builds a product, seeks its market and answers to the funds that remain?

> [!WARNING] Sketch
> This profile tests the generality of the kernel. No use has validated its values.

## Streams

| Stream | Role | Regime | Capacity |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Product | production | `planned` | share |
| Internal tooling | tooling | `pulled` | ceiling |
| Customer and market discovery | survey | `floor` | floor, in fixed sessions of interviews |
| Skills | training | `floor` | floor |
| Investors and partners | exchange | `appointed` | cap per meeting |
| Administration | upkeep | `batched` | slots |
| Customer support | exchange | `reactive` | reserve; kinds outage (`immediate`) and question (`windowed`) |

## Names

## Options

| Option | Setting |
| --- | --- |
| Reactive work | on, medium |
| External parties | on: customers (`consumes`, `requests`), contractors (`delegates`), investors (`reviews`) |
| Imposed deadlines | on: the end of the funds is a `hard` deadline on the purpose itself; launches promised to customers are deadlines on deliverables |
| Valuation | quantified: revenue for the value; the cost of capacity rises as the funds run out |

## Notes specific to the domain

- **The runway is a hard deadline.** Its date is the funds divided by the burn rate, revised by the agent at each cycle planning. When the test fails, the third [adjustment](../kernel/objects/deadline.md#^adjustment-order) is to cut the burn or raise funds, levers that lie outside the allocation of attention and are recorded as such.
- **The phase governs the tooling.** Tooling built during problem discovery serves a product that may not be wanted. The [pull rule](../kernel/processes/tooling.md#^pull-rule) admits only the means that an experiment of discovery consumes.
- **Discovery is a floor, not a phase that ends.** The sessions with customers continue during scaling, since the stream of survey is the one that building displaces.
- **The stop by value applies fully.** The [checkpoint](../kernel/processes/uncertainty-control.md#^stop-by-value) compares the expected worth of one more interval with its cost at the current cost of capacity.
