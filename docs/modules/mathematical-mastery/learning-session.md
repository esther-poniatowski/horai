# Learning session

> [!QUESTION] Goal: how is one session of mathematical training conducted without automated agents?

The session applies the theory of [mastery](mastery.md).

1. **Recall.** Reproduce on a blank page the central proof of a concept studied at least one week earlier. If the reproduction succeeds, close the concept.
2. **Item.** Choose the next item of the [competency graph](mastery.md#^competency-graph): a proof obligation of an active deliverable first, a foundational item otherwise.
3. **Work.** Do one of three activities: a reproduction, exercises, or the verification of a derivation that an automated agent produced, with an explanation of each step.
4. **Record.** Note the points of failure, and schedule the recall of the item for a session at least one week later.

> [!NOTE]
> Automated agents are consulted only after the session, to check a result or to locate a source.
