# Mastery

> [!QUESTION] Goal: how is mathematical training organized, so that delegation to automated agents does not replace the understanding of the agent?

## What to learn

The concepts that the deliverables require form a small **competency graph**, ordered by prerequisite. Two tracks draw on it. ^competency-graph

- **Just in time.** Items are derived from the proof obligations of the active deliverables and of their coming milestones.
- **Foundational.** Items that several deliverables presuppose are studied on the floor of the stream.

## How to practice

The sessions exclude automated agents. Three activities fill them:

- reproduction of a key result on a blank page;
- exercises;
- verification, line by line, of a derivation that an automated agent produced, with an explanation of each step. The verification turns delegated work into training material.

These activities apply retrieval practice, spacing and self-explanation, whose support the note on [learning science](../../references/learning-science.md) rates.

## Criterion of mastery

A concept is closed when its central proof is reproduced without support after a delay of at least one week. ^mastery-criterion

> [!WARNING] Extrapolation
> The evidence covers undergraduate mathematics. The delay of one week and the criterion itself extend it to the level of research without a direct test.

The steps of a session are given in the [procedure for a learning session](learning-session.md).
