# Modules

States the processes that serve the streams of one [yield](../kernel/objects/stream.md#^roles) in particular domains. A module fills the sessions of a stream with a method, and the kernel gives that stream its allocation.

> [!IMPORTANT] Form of a module
> Each module names what the stream that it serves yields, the trigger that it presupposes and the profiles that use it. It holds a note of theory and the procedure of one session. It depends on the kernel alone, as the [layers](../kernel/foundations/layers.md) require.

- [Bibliography](bibliography/_index.md): serves a stream that yields knowledge, where the environment is a literature
- [Mathematical mastery](mathematical-mastery/_index.md): serves a stream that yields competence, where that competence is mathematical

> [!NOTE] Modules not yet written
> The research profile calls for four further modules, ratified as proposals: writing, delegation (the preparation of a brief and the review of its output), meeting preparation, and experiments and simulations. The sketched profiles call for analogous modules, such as the interviews of customers for the stream of discovery of a founder. None is written.
