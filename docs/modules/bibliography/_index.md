# Bibliography

Serves the role **survey** under the regime `floor`. Reading that one milestone needs is charged to that milestone, as the [rule of one regime per stream](../../kernel/objects/stream.md#^streams) requires. Used by the [research profile](../../profiles/research.md), and applicable to any profile whose environment is a literature.

- [Reading pipeline](reading-pipeline.md): directs reading by the questions of each deliverable, through a bounded pipeline
- [Reading session](reading-session.md): conducts one session, from the choice of the paper to the written artifact
