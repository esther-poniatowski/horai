# Reading session

> [!QUESTION] Goal: how is one session of bibliography conducted, from the choice of the paper to the written artifact?

The session applies the [reading pipeline](reading-pipeline.md).

1. **Question.** Choose one positioning question of an active deliverable. In a surveillance session, take the triaged alerts instead.
2. **Paper.** Take a paper of the stage `triaged` that bears on the question. If three papers are already in the stage `read`, finish one of them first.
3. **Depth.** Choose the depth: a decision in ten minutes, a structured note in one hour, or a reproduction.
4. **Read.** Read at the chosen depth, and stop at its limit of time.
5. **Artifact.** Write a paragraph of related work, or a note linked to the claim that the paper supports, contests or supplies with a method.
6. **Update.** Move the paper to the stage `synthesized`, and close or refine the positioning question.
