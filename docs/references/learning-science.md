# Learning science

## Findings

- **Spaced retrieval.** Hopkins and co-authors report that retrieval practice spaced over a course improves the retention of mathematics in an engineering calculus course. The material is procedural [hopkins2016].
- **Self-explanation.** Hodds, Alcock and Inglis report that a training in self-explanation improves the comprehension of proofs by undergraduates, with an effect of $d = 0.95$ in the laboratory and of $d = 0.35$ after 20 days in a course [hodds2014]. The study does not test retrieval or spacing.

No study was found at the level of research mathematics.

## Bearing on `horai`

- The reproduction on a blank page applies retrieval, the delay of one week applies spacing, and the explained verification of a derivation applies self-explanation, in the process of [mathematical mastery](../modules/mathematical-mastery/mastery.md).
- The [criterion of mastery](../modules/mathematical-mastery/mastery.md#^mastery-criterion) extrapolates these findings beyond the populations studied.
