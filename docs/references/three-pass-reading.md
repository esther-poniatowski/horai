# Three-pass reading

## Method

Keshav describes the reading of a paper in three passes [keshav2007]:

1. a first pass of a few minutes over the title, the abstract, the headings and the conclusions, which decides whether to read further;
2. a second pass of about an hour, which grasps the content without the details of the proofs;
3. a third pass, which reconstructs the paper from its assumptions.

The source is a paper of advice, and reports no test of the method.

## Bearing on `horai`

- The three depths of the [reading pipeline](../modules/bibliography/reading-pipeline.md#^pipeline) correspond to the three passes.
- In `horai`, the first pass is prepared by agents, and the third is reserved for a paper whose method is reused.

## Sources

- [keshav2007] ○ Keshav, "How to read a paper", *ACM SIGCOMM Computer Communication Review*, 37(3), 2007, pp. 83–84
