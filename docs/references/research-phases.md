# Phases of research

## Account

Nanda describes the conduct of a research project in phases with different aims [nanda2025]:

- **Exploration** aims at information. The researcher does not yet know which claim to establish, and the absence of a clear next step is normal. Fast feedback loops and flexible tooling matter most in this phase.
- **Understanding** aims at testing definite hypotheses until a claim is established.
- **Distillation** aims at a written account of the established claims.

Two further sources address the choice of a problem, which precedes these phases: Alon on the choice of a scientific problem [alon2009], and Hamming on important problems [hamming1986].

## Bearing on `horai`

- The three [phases](../kernel/objects/phase.md#^phases) of the kernel generalize this account, whose names the research profile keeps, and the decision on [phase-dependent planning](../adr/phase-dependent-planning.md) restricts milestones to the last two.
- The weight given to tooling in exploration supports the [constraint rule](../kernel/processes/enablement.md#^constraint-rule) for a blocking tool.
- The method does not address the choice of a problem. The strategic review of the ideas file is the place where the two further sources would apply.
