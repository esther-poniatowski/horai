# References

Gathers the external methodologies and findings on which the method of `horai` draws. Each note states its source on its own terms, then its bearing on the method.

## Methodologies of planning

- [Shape Up](shape-up.md): fixes the time of a piece of work and lets its scope vary
- [Kanban and Lean pull](kanban-and-lean-pull.md): separates an inventory from a committed set, and limits the work in progress
- [Critical path and theory of constraints](critical-path-and-constraints.md): orders a known network of activities, and directs effort to the constraint of a system
- [Portfolio and phase frameworks](portfolio-and-phase-frameworks.md): allocates resources by class of work, and types a situation by what is known of it

## Results on ordering and queues

- [Cost of delay](cost-of-delay.md): orders the items of one queue by the value that their delay destroys
- [Queueing and reactive work](queueing-and-reactive-work.md): relates the delay of a request to the utilization of the capacity that serves it

## Findings on duration and attention

- [Distributions of duration](duration-distributions.md): reports the laws that the durations of tasks and the overruns of projects follow
- [Attention and switching](attention-and-switching.md): reports the cost of a change of task, and the evidence on the length of a session

## Practices of particular domains

- [Phases of research](research-phases.md): separates exploration, understanding and distillation in the conduct of a research project
- [Three-pass reading](three-pass-reading.md): reads a paper at three successive depths
- [Learning science](learning-science.md): reports the effects of retrieval, spacing and self-explanation on the learning of mathematics
- [AI delegation](ai-delegation.md): reports the measured effect of AI assistance on experienced developers
