# Portfolio and phase frameworks

## Allocation by class

- **Strategic buckets.** In the management of a portfolio of new products, Cooper and co-authors divide the resources among classes of projects before any project is ranked, so that long-term projects are not displaced by short-term ones within one ranked list [cooper2001].
- **Production and production capability.** Covey distinguishes what is produced from the capacity to produce, and holds that effectiveness lies in the balance of the two [covey1989].

## Typing by what is known

- **Cynefin.** Snowden and Boone type a situation as simple, complicated, complex or chaotic, and prescribe a different mode of decision in each: in a complex situation, one probes before one analyses [snowden2007]. Later versions of the framework rename the simple domain clear.
- **Double diamond.** The Design Council describes a design process in four stages, discover, define, develop and deliver, which alternate divergence and convergence [designcouncil2005].
- **Lean Startup.** Ries separates the search for a product that customers want, conducted by experiments, from its scaling [ries2011].

## Bearing on `horai`

- The [allocation of capacity](../kernel/processes/capacity-allocation.md) by stream is a form of strategic buckets for one agent, and the roles sensing and capability are forms of production capability.
- The three [phases](../kernel/objects/phase.md#^phases) of the kernel correspond to these frameworks as follows:

| Kernel | Cynefin | Double diamond | Lean Startup |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Exploration | complex | discover, define | search for the problem |
| Validation | complicated | develop | test of the solution |
| Delivery | clear | deliver | scaling |

- The correspondences are approximate: each framework was stated for organizations, and none for the attention of one person.
